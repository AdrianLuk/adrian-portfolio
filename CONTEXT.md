# adrian-portfolio

Adrian Luk's public portfolio at adrianluk.com. It exists to win senior frontend roles by showing, not just describing, his craft.

## Language

**Project**:
A piece of work Adrian did, at a job or on his own, such as Control D, Life House, BT Cup, Studio or Juice Bros.
_Avoid_: Job, client work, portfolio piece, "btcup" (write BT Cup)

**Case study**:
The write-up of one Project on its own page: problem, what Adrian did, approach and outcome. Side projects qualify just like employer work.
_Avoid_: Work item, project page, portfolio piece

**Highlight**:
A short entry on the home page for one Project: a verified paragraph and key numbers. It links to the Project's Case study if it has one, otherwise to its Role on the Resume page.
_Avoid_: Card, teaser, work item

**Role**:
A position Adrian held, with company, title and dates, such as Senior Software Engineer at Control D (Windscribe). A Role can include several Projects.
_Avoid_: Job, position, experience entry

**Side project**:
A Project Adrian runs on his own, outside any Role, such as Juice Bros. It is listed in its own group on the Resume page, never in the Role timeline.
_Avoid_: Personal project, freelance

**Player tools**:
The free Juice Bros tools for pickleball players, such as Booking Buddy, Pickle Point Pal, Match Mixer and Drum Roll. The set grows, so never state a count, and not all are no-login (Booking Buddy needs one). Public, and showcased in the Juice Bros Case study.
_Avoid_: Tools (unqualified), apps

**Private tools**:
Adrian's personal tools at tools.adrianluk.com, such as the blurbs app. The portfolio never shows or links to them.
_Avoid_: Tools (unqualified), dashboard

**Resume page**:
The page listing every Role, then Side projects, with verified bullets, and offering the Resume PDF as a download.
_Avoid_: Experience page, CV page

**Resume PDF**:
The downloadable resume file, offered as a PDF and as a Word (.docx) copy. Both and the Resume page state the same facts and must be kept in sync.
_Avoid_: CV
